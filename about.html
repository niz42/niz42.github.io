About

Kailun Ni / 倪凯伦 — HCI & Serious Game Researcher

Profile

I am a recent MSc graduate in Design Informatics from the University of Edinburgh, with a BSc in Information Management and Information Systems from Central South University. My research sits at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Serious Games, exploring how gameful experiences can support learning, well-being, and behavioral change.

I am especially interested in designing playful, evidence-based interventions using mixed-methods research — combining qualitative user studies, prototyping, and quantitative evaluation to understand how people engage with interactive systems.
